Open-Meteo
When live weather is available, Astronomy Observer requests forecast and air-quality values directly from Open-Meteo. API data are offered under CC BY 4.0. Astronomy Observer rounds the requested location and combines or transforms weather values into derived observing scores; the original provider is shown in Source status.
Data from Open-Meteo.
https://open-meteo.com/
https://open-meteo.com/en/license
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

MET Norway
MET Norway Locationforecast is used as an independent weather fallback. Requests identify Astronomy Observer with a project User-Agent and use reduced coordinate precision. Weather values are combined or transformed into derived observing scores; the original provider is shown in Source status.
Data from MET Norway. Unless a product says otherwise, MET Norway documents its open products under NLOD 2.0 and CC BY 4.0.
https://api.met.no/weatherapi/locationforecast/2.0/documentation
https://api.met.no/doc/License
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Other live astronomy sources
Current visual-satellite elements come directly from CelesTrak, current comet elements from the IAU Minor Planet Center, and aurora data from NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center. These changing datasets are downloaded only when the relevant feature is enabled and cached locally. They are not bundled into the APK.
https://celestrak.org/
https://www.minorplanetcenter.net/
https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/products/aurora-30-minute-forecast

Meteor-shower planning data
The bundled major-shower table is based on recurring information published by the International Meteor Organization. It is a planning aid; the current IMO calendar remains the reference for exact annual circumstances.
https://www.imo.net/resources/calendar/
